When to Start
Begin at Tranche 1 (−10%). Shallow corrections often recover without hitting deeper levels — do not wait for the perfect bottom.
Sweet Spot
Tranches 3 and 4 (−20% to −25%) are historically the best value zone — deep enough for strong recovery gains, but common enough to actually occur.
Tranche 5 (−30%)
The biggest, deepest bullet (30% of dry powder). Reserve it for genuine crises — GFC-level events. If it never triggers, your earlier tranches already did the job. Patience is the strategy.
